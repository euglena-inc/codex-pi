# Two complete deliveries with whole-task Codex analysis

Codex's primary contribution is whole-task design and review: freed from routine
implementation, it examines shared causes, difficult boundaries and later risks.
Pi implements, integrates, tests and repairs ordinary failures autonomously.

Before dispatch, Codex verifies the decision-critical source and contracts, thinks
through the full authorized outcome and remaining plan, resolves design choices,
and gives Pi an executable result with acceptance evidence and bounded scope.
The model chooses the research and implementation path; do not prescribe a fixed
matrix, mandatory headings, function list or step-by-step workflow.

After the first reviewed quality failure, the same Codex main session reassesses
both the complete outcome and the remaining authorized plan. Consolidate all
reachable affected paths and shared causes, relevant state/ordering boundaries,
evaluation validity and downstream dependencies. Resolve what the evidence
allows; record genuine unknowns. Produce one coherent complete repair solution
in the existing design and the next immutable task brief. Continue the SAME Pi
session/worktree for a second complete delivery.

If the second delivery also fails quality review, Codex repeats that whole-task
analysis, verifies writer release, then directly implements and verifies in the
existing main session. Do not dispatch a third Pi delivery of the failed outcome,
rename it to reset the count, weaken acceptance or reset budgets.

Previous takeovers repeatedly found omissions after green component checks
because the main session stopped at the current defect. Source-backed analysis
must cover the affected outcome and likely later problems, not just the reported
symptom. Consolidate repairs; use relevant targeted checks, then verify the frozen
candidate with required final checks. Missing/unknown required evidence remains
blocking. Preserve single-use evaluation and budget constraints.
